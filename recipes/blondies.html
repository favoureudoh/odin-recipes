strawberry-lemon-blondies

Description

This powerhouse combination of fresh berries and punchy citrus that tastes just like strawberry lemonade

Ingredient

  • 1 cup salted butter,softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 large lemon
  • 2 1/3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 1/4 cups diced fresh strawberry

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven. Line a baking dish with lightly greased
  2. Beat butter and sugar together unti creamy
  3. Zest the lemon; cut lemon in half,squeeze, and measure 3 tablespoon lemon juice. Add zest juice to blondie and beat until fully incorporated
  4. Spread batter into the baking dish and smooth the top
