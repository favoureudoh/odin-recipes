classic-meatloaf

Description

When you're in need of delicious comfort food that every houseload, classic-meatloaf is the way to go

It is a classic meal and an incredible simple one

Ingredient

  • Ground Chuck
  • Vegetables
  • Worcestershire Sauce
  • Egg
  • Spices
  • Bread Crumbs
  • Glaze

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredient
  2. Preheat the oven. Line a baking dish with lightly greased
  3. Place carrot, celery, onion, bell pepper, mushrooms, garlic in a food processor, Transfer for a large mixing bowl
  4. Bake, remove meatloaf,return to oven, cooking time will depend on shape and thickness of the meatloaf
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!
